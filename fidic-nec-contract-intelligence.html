THE CHALLENGE

Standard forms are familiar — but project-specific amendments and procedures change the answer.

FIDIC and NEC contracts contain structured mechanisms for notices, claims, early warning, change, time and payment. Those mechanisms must be read together with project-specific amendments and the facts of the live project.

FIDIC & NEC Contract Intelligence helps teams navigate those relationships without losing the contractual context behind the provision.
