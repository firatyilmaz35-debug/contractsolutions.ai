THE CHALLENGE

The real difficulty is not finding a clause — it is understanding the contractual path that follows from it.

FIDIC and NEC contracts operate through linked procedures: notices trigger responses, instructions affect entitlement, time limits influence rights, defined roles determine authority, and amended provisions can materially change the standard mechanism.

Project teams often work across the General Conditions, Particular Conditions, Contract Data, Z clauses, schedules, Employer’s Requirements and other contract documents at the same time. Contract intelligence should therefore explain not only what a clause says, but how the relevant provisions interact, what must happen next and which project records support the position.
