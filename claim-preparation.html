THE CHALLENGE

Claims are weakened by broken links — not by a lack of documents.

A contractual clause on its own does not establish a claim. A strong submission must show what happened, why the contract allocates the relevant risk, whether procedural requirements were met, how the event caused the alleged consequence and what relief is properly supported.

Those elements are usually scattered across General and Particular Conditions, bespoke amendments, instructions, letters, meeting minutes, site records, programmes, cost records and expert workstreams. Notice requirements and time bars may also operate differently under amended or bespoke contracts.

Claim Preparation is designed to connect those sources into a controlled claim structure. It helps teams identify missing links early, preserve source traceability and organise the material needed for contractual, delay, quantum and legal review before the claim is finalised.

KEY CAPABILITIES

Build the claim around entitlement, procedure, facts, evidence and impact.

01

Contractual Entitlement Mapping

Map each potential claim head to the relevant contractual provisions, risk allocation and available relief. The review can follow FIDIC, NEC, amended standard forms or bespoke claim mechanisms, including Particular Conditions and project-specific amendments.

02

Notice & Procedural Compliance

Identify triggering events, notice periods, particulars, record-keeping requirements, update obligations, response periods and final-submission requirements. Structure the procedural history so compliance issues and missing steps can be reviewed before submission.

03

Event & Chronology Reconstruction

Build a dated sequence from instructions, correspondence, meeting minutes, reports and project records. Separate the underlying event from later communications and identify the milestones that matter to entitlement, causation and contractual procedure.

04

Evidence Mapping & Source Traceability

Connect key factual propositions to the documents and records that support them. Organise source references by issue, event and claim component, while highlighting assertions that still require stronger or more contemporaneous evidence.

05

Causation & Impact Framework

Structure the logic from event to consequence: what occurred, which contractual risk was engaged, what activity, scope or resource was affected, and what time or cost consequence is alleged. Specialist delay and quantum analysis can then be integrated into that framework.

06

Claim Heads, Relief & Reservations

Separate and organise the relief being advanced — including time, prolongation, disruption, change-related cost, payment or other contractual remedies — and maintain clear reservations where effects are continuing, incomplete or subject to later specialist assessment.

07

Claim Narrative & Submission Assembly

Develop a coherent submission structure covering contractual background, facts, notices, entitlement, causation, impact, relief and supporting appendices. Keep the narrative tied to underlying clauses and project records rather than producing an unsupported summary.

08

Specialist Interface & Consistency Control

Coordinate contractual drafting with planning, quantum, commercial, technical and legal inputs. Check that dates, clause references, factual positions, assumptions, amounts and requested relief remain consistent across the main claim and supporting workstreams.

WHERE IT FITS

Use the claim workflow from the first event — not only when the submission is due.

Claim Preparation is most useful when entitlement, records and procedure are controlled progressively. The same structure can then support interim submissions, detailed claims and later review or negotiation.

01

Early Event Screening

Assess a developing event against the contract and identify possible entitlement, notice obligations, evidence needs and immediate actions.

02

Notice & Record Preservation

Keep contractual notices, continuing particulars and contemporaneous records aligned while the event and its effects are still developing.

03

Interim & Rolling Claims

Maintain a controlled claim position where entitlement or effects need to be updated periodically before the full impact is known.

04

Detailed / Final Submission

Consolidate contractual, factual, delay, quantum and evidential material into a structured claim package for professional review and submission.

05

Review, Revision & Negotiation

Revisit rejected, partially determined or negotiated claims, identify evidential or logic gaps and organise the material needed for the next contractual step.

TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Working outputs that make the claim easier to review, challenge and complete.

Outputs are structured around the underlying contract and project records so the professional team can see the basis of each position, the supporting source and any outstanding gap.

01

Claim Strategy & Issue Map

A management-level view of the event, potential claim heads, contractual routes, key procedural issues, principal evidence and actions still required.

02

Entitlement Matrix

Claim heads mapped to relevant clauses, Particular Conditions or bespoke provisions, risk allocation, contractual preconditions and relief potentially available.

03

Notice & Procedure Register

A dated register of notices, triggers, required particulars, responses, update obligations, submission periods and procedural status.

04

Event Chronology

A source-referenced timeline of instructions, correspondence, decisions, site events and contractual milestones relevant to the claim.

05

Evidence & Source Index

Supporting documents and contemporaneous records organised by issue and linked to the factual propositions or claim components they support.

06

Causation & Impact Matrix

A working map connecting each event to the alleged consequence, affected scope or activity, evidence relied upon and required delay or quantum input.

07

Claim Submission Framework

A structured draft or review framework covering contractual background, facts, notices, entitlement, causation, impact, relief and supporting appendices.

08

Gap & Specialist Input Schedule

An action list identifying missing records, unresolved contractual points and the planning, quantum, technical or legal input still required before finalisation.

Contract Solutions AI supports professional claim preparation and review. It does not replace legal advice, forensic delay analysis, quantum assessment, technical expert opinion or the judgment of qualified contract and claims professionals.

WORKFLOW

From project event to controlled claim submission.

The workflow keeps the contract and source record visible while the team develops the claim, tests procedural compliance, integrates specialist analysis and prepares the material for professional approval.

01

Define

Set the contract, claiming or reviewing party, event, claim type, relevant cut-off date and relief being considered.

02

Build the Record

Add the relevant clauses, amendments, notices, correspondence, instructions, reports and contemporaneous project records.

03

Map & Validate

Test entitlement, procedure, chronology, evidence and cause-effect logic; identify gaps and integrate planning, quantum or legal inputs where needed.

04

Prepare & Control

Assemble the narrative, matrices, schedules and appendices, then check consistency and source support before professional approval and submission.
