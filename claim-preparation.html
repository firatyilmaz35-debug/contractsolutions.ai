THE CHALLENGE

A claim is only as strong as the link between entitlement, causation and evidence.

Strong claims require more than a chronology. They need a clear contractual basis, properly identified cause-and-effect relationships, timely notices and records that support the position being advanced.

Claim Preparation structures those elements so contract teams can identify gaps earlier and build submissions around a defensible contractual narrative.
