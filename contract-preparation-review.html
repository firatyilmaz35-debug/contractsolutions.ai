THE CHALLENGE

Contract review should identify what matters — not just summarise the document.

Knowing what the contract says is not enough. Teams need to understand what has changed, who must act, by when, and where the risk sits.

Complex project contracts are rarely contained in a single document. General Conditions, Particular Conditions, Employer’s Requirements, schedules, amendments, addenda, clarifications and bespoke provisions may overlap, modify one another or create inconsistencies.

A seemingly minor amendment can materially change risk allocation, notice requirements, payment mechanisms, time entitlement or the procedure for administering change. An effective contract review should therefore identify departures, obligations, dependencies and deadlines — and convert them into a practical framework the project team can use.
