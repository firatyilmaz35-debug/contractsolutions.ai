THE CHALLENGE

Subcontract risk is created at award but usually becomes visible only when scope, payment, time or change starts to go wrong.

A subcontract is not simply a shorter version of the main contract. The contractor needs to decide which upstream obligations must flow down, which risks should remain with the main contractor, how scope and interfaces are divided, how payment and securities operate, and what happens when instructions, delay or change occur.

After award, those drafting decisions need to become a live administration system. If the subcontract, purchase documents, correspondence, notices, variations, progress, payment and close-out records are managed separately, the project can lose both upstream recovery and downstream contractual control.

TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Outputs that connect the upstream contract, downstream package and live project record.

The agent is intended to make subcontract administration consistent across procurement, contract, commercial and site teams while keeping professional approval and negotiation with the responsible users.

01

Subcontract Draft / Review Pack

A structured subcontract package or review schedule covering key conditions, project particulars, scope, schedules, departures and matters requiring negotiation or approval.

02

Main-to-Subcontract Flow-Down Matrix

Relevant main-contract obligations mapped to the proposed subcontract wording, with gaps, conflicts and non-back-to-back positions identified for review.

03

Scope & Interface Matrix

Responsibilities, exclusions, dependencies, design interfaces, deliverables and handover points organised by party and work package.

04

Obligations & Deliverables Register

Subcontractor and contractor obligations, submissions, approvals, milestones, records and deadlines mapped to the relevant contractual references.

05

Commercial & Payment Schedule

Rates, quantities, milestones, certification steps, retention, advance-payment recovery, deductions and other payment controls organised for administration.

06

Notice & Time-Bar Register

Subcontract notices, response periods, EOT/claim deadlines, payment notices and other procedural dates tracked by event and responsible team.

07

Variation / Claim Register

Instructions, variations, quotations, EOT requests, claims, assessments, agreements and unresolved items maintained in a structured downstream change record.

08

Close-Out & Security Checklist

Completion, defects, final account, warranties, as-built documents, guarantees, retention and release conditions tracked through final subcontract close-out.
