THE CHALLENGE

Complex disputes become expensive when the legal theory, project facts and evidentiary record are built in separate silos.

Construction disputes may involve years of correspondence, programme files, payment records, technical documents, notices, meeting minutes, claim submissions, determinations and expert analyses. The same event may appear differently across contractual, factual, delay, quantum and technical workstreams.

A useful dispute-support system should make those relationships reviewable. It should help the team identify what is alleged, what must be proved, which documents support or contradict the position, what expert input is still missing and how the chronology connects with the pleaded or negotiated case.

KEY CAPABILITIES

Build a dispute record that is structured by issue, evidence and proof — not just folders.

01

Issue Tree & Case Theory Mapping

Break the dispute into claims, counterclaims, defences, contractual issues, factual propositions and required elements of proof for counsel and expert review.

02

Master Chronology Reconstruction

Build a source-linked chronology from correspondence, notices, instructions, minutes, programmes, reports and other project records, with the ability to view events by issue or workstream.

03

Evidence & Citation Mapping

Connect propositions and disputed facts with the supporting or contradictory source documents so the team can trace the evidentiary basis behind each position.

04

Claim / Defence Position Matrix

Map each party’s stated position, contractual basis, factual allegations, relief sought, responses, admissions, disputed points and evidentiary gaps in one working structure.

05

Witness & Fact Development Support

Organise factual topics, contemporaneous records and potential witness knowledge to support lawyer-led witness preparation without generating or replacing witness evidence.

06

Expert Workstream Coordination

Coordinate delay, quantum, technical and other expert inputs by issue, required source documents, assumptions, dependencies and outstanding questions.

07

Document Review & Production Support

Structure potentially relevant document populations, review status, issue tags and document requests or production workstreams subject to the applicable procedural rules and counsel direction.

08

Procedural & Submission Readiness

Track key procedural dates, requested inputs, open evidence, expert dependencies and submission components so the team can see what is required before the next procedural milestone.

TYPICAL OUTPUTS

A common evidence architecture for lawyers, experts and the project team.

The objective is to reduce duplicated review and make every important proposition traceable back to the contemporaneous project record.

01

Dispute Issues Matrix

Claims, counterclaims, defences, disputed propositions, contractual grounds, relief and responsibility for each workstream organised in one master issue map.

02

Master Event Chronology

A source-linked timeline of the project and disputed events, filterable by issue, party, contractual process, delay event or other workstream.

03

Evidence & Source Index

Key documents mapped to the facts or issues they support, contradict or contextualise, together with review notes and outstanding evidentiary gaps.

04

Claim / Defence Comparison

The parties’ competing positions presented side by side with references to pleadings, submissions, notices, determinations and supporting records.

05

Witness Fact Matrix

Potential factual topics, contemporaneous documents and witness knowledge areas organised for counsel-led interview and statement preparation.

06

Expert Input Coordination Schedule

Questions, source documents, assumptions and dependencies allocated across delay, quantum, technical and other expert workstreams.

07

Document Review / Production Log

Document populations, search or review status, issue coding and request/production tracking organised under the procedural approach directed by counsel.

08

Submission & Hearing Preparation Pack

An organised working pack of issues, chronologies, evidence, expert dependencies and open actions supporting counsel and experts before submissions, hearings or settlement discussions.

Contract Solutions AI is a dispute-support and information-organisation tool. It does not provide legal advice, make legal determinations, replace counsel, prepare independent expert opinions or replace witness evidence. All legal strategy, submissions, expert conclusions and procedural decisions remain subject to qualified professional review.

WHERE IT FITS

Useful before a dispute crystallises and throughout formal dispute proceedings.

The earlier the evidence architecture is built, the less time the team spends reconstructing the project record after positions have hardened.

01

02

Dispute Board / Adjudication

03

Negotiation & Mediation

Support settlement analysis by clarifying disputed propositions, evidentiary strengths, expert dependencies and areas capable of narrowing.

04

Arbitration Preparation

Coordinate counsel, project witnesses and expert workstreams across large document sets, pleadings, chronologies and procedural milestones.

05

Hearing & Case Management

Maintain an issue-centred record of evidence, witness topics, expert materials and open actions as the case develops toward or through a hearing.
