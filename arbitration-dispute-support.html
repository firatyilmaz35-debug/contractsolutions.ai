THE CHALLENGE

Disputes become harder to manage when the project record is fragmented across thousands of documents.

By the time a project issue develops into formal proceedings, the relevant evidence may be spread across correspondence, notices, programmes, reports, expert material and commercial records.

Arbitration & Dispute Support helps structure that material around the issues in dispute so legal, contract and expert teams can navigate the record more efficiently.
