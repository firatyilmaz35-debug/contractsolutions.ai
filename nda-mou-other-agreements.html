THE CHALLENGE

“Short-form” agreements can create long-term obligations when purpose, authority, confidentiality, exclusivity or exit rights are not clear.

NDAs, MOUs and preliminary commercial agreements are often signed early, under time pressure and before the full project structure is settled. Yet they may contain binding confidentiality duties, exclusivity, IP provisions, cost commitments, governing-law clauses, non-solicitation terms or obligations to negotiate and cooperate.

The review therefore needs to separate what is intended to be binding from what is aspirational, identify missing protections and approvals, and make the agreement’s operational obligations visible after signature. The same principle applies to other project-related commercial agreements configured within the platform.

TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Clear working outputs for drafting, negotiation, approval and post-signature control.

The solution is intended to speed up first-pass preparation and review while making departures and decision points easier for contract and legal teams to validate.

01

Agreement Draft / Review Schedule

A structured working version or review schedule identifying proposed wording, open issues and matters requiring commercial or legal approval.

02

Key Terms Summary

Parties, purpose, duration, binding status, scope, payment, confidentiality, IP, liability, termination and other principal terms captured in one management view.

03

Risk & Deviation Register

Departures from the approved template or playbook organised by clause, risk area, significance, proposed response and responsible reviewer.

04

Binding / Non-Binding Matrix

For preliminary agreements, provisions classified for review according to whether they are intended to create binding obligations, survive termination or remain statements of intention.

05

Obligations & Governance Matrix

Action owners, approvals, cooperation requirements, deliverables, decision rights, confidentiality duties and other operational obligations mapped to the agreement.

06

Negotiation & Approval Schedule

Open drafting points, counterpart positions, internal approvals and fallback positions organised for negotiation and signature workflow.

07

Renewal / Expiry & Notice Tracker

Effective dates, expiry, automatic renewal, termination windows, notice requirements and surviving obligations tracked after execution.

08

Agreement Register

A portfolio-level view of configured project-related agreements, counterparties, status, owners, key dates and outstanding actions.
