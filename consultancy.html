CONSULTANCY

Expert contract and claims consultancy, supported by AI.

Contract Solutions AI combines specialist contract management expertise with AI-powered workflows to support complex projects from contract formation through claims, delay, change and dispute preparation.

EXPERTISE + TECHNOLOGY

Professional judgment where the project needs it.

Some teams need software. Others need experienced professionals to take ownership of a defined contractual workstream. Complex matters often require both.

Our consultancy services are designed to work independently or alongside the Contract Solutions AI platform, combining structured technology-enabled workflows with project-specific professional analysis and review.

CONSULTANCY SERVICES

Support across the contract lifecycle.

01

Contract Preparation & Review

Tender-stage and pre-execution contract review, risk allocation, bespoke provisions, FIDIC/NEC amendments, departures, clarification schedules and negotiation support.

02

Contract & Commercial Management

Ongoing contract administration, correspondence strategy, notices, contractual compliance, obligations, payment procedures, records and commercial support during project delivery.

03

Claims Preparation & Assessment

Claim strategy, entitlement analysis, procedural compliance, chronology, evidence structuring, causation, substantiation and preparation or independent assessment of contractual claims.

04

Delay & EOT Advisory

Programme and delay-event review, EOT strategy, baseline/update analysis and support involving planning information from Oracle Primavera P6, Microsoft Project and other project controls sources.

05

Variation & Change Management

Change identification, instruction review, contractual entitlement, notification, valuation strategy, programme interfaces, substantiation and variation/change-order submissions.

06

Subcontract Management

Subcontract preparation and review, main-contract flow-down, scope and interface management, notices, payment, variations, claims, performance issues and close-out.

07

Contract Health Checks & Audits

Independent review of active projects to identify contractual exposure, missed or upcoming notices, unrecorded change, potential claims, documentation gaps and administration weaknesses.

08

Dispute & Arbitration Support

Issue and chronology development, claim/defence preparation, document and evidence organisation, and coordination with legal counsel, delay, quantum and technical experts.

ENGAGEMENT APPROACH

Defined scope. Traceable work. Practical outputs.

01

Understand the matter

Clarify the contract, project status, issue, available records, required output and decision or deadline the work needs to support.

02

Define the scope

Agree the consultancy workstream, responsibilities, information requirements, specialist interfaces and deliverables.

03

Analyse & develop

Review the contractual and project evidence, develop the required analysis and use AI-enabled workflows where they improve speed, consistency or traceability.

04

Review & deliver

Professionally validate the work, identify assumptions or gaps and provide outputs suitable for the agreed management, negotiation, claim or dispute purpose.

PROFESSIONAL INTERFACES

Where a matter requires formal legal advice, expert testimony, independent quantum opinion, specialist delay expert opinion or other regulated/specialist services, we can structure our work to interface with the relevant appointed professionals rather than presenting AI-assisted analysis as a substitute for their role.
