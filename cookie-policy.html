Cookie Policy

This policy explains the use of cookies and analytics technologies on the Contract Solutions AI public website.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. Analytics

Contract Solutions AI uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the public website, identify technical or content issues and improve the user experience. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the visitor's browser, device and consent settings.

Analytics information may include pages visited, interaction data, referral source, device and browser information and approximate geographic information. We do not use this information to sell personal data or build advertising profiles.

2. Essential technologies

Technical processing may also occur through the browser, hosting infrastructure and security mechanisms that are necessary to deliver and protect the website. These technologies are not used by us to track visitors across unrelated websites.

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential analytics cookies or comparable technologies, appropriate consent controls should be used before such technologies are activated for a visitor.

3. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website and its analytics configuration develop. The latest version will be published on this page.

4. Contact
