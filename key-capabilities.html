KEY CAPABILITIES

Contract intelligence built around professional workflows.

Across every solution, Contract Solutions AI is designed to connect contractual language, project records, programme information and professional review into structured, traceable workflows.

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

More than document summarisation.

The platform is intended to help experienced teams find, compare, organise and validate the information that drives contract administration, claims and project decisions. AI accelerates the work; professional judgment remains in control.

01

Contract & Document Intelligence

Analyse Conditions of Contract, Particular Conditions, bespoke agreements, schedules, appendices, amendments, correspondence and project records in contractual context.

02

Clause & Amendment Comparison

Compare standard wording, Particular Conditions, negotiated amendments and bespoke provisions to identify material departures and their practical effect.

03

Obligations, Notices & Time Bars

Structure party obligations, triggers, notice requirements, submission periods, response times, deliverables and consequences of non-compliance.

04

Cross-Document Consistency

Identify conflicts, hierarchy issues, definition mismatches, gaps and inconsistencies across interconnected contract and project documents.

05

Risk & Entitlement Mapping

Connect facts and contractual provisions to potential risk allocation, entitlement pathways, procedural requirements and areas requiring professional review.

06

Chronology & Event Structuring

Organise instructions, notices, correspondence, approvals, programme events and contemporaneous records into issue-specific chronologies.

07

Evidence & Source Traceability

Link findings and drafted positions back to underlying clauses, correspondence, records and supporting documents so professional users can verify the source.

08

Programme Intelligence

Support review of baseline and update information from planning environments such as Oracle Primavera P6 and Microsoft Project, alongside delay events and contractual records.

09

Structured Professional Outputs

Convert reviewed information into registers, matrices, chronologies, issue schedules, draft narratives and decision-ready working documents.

10

Human Review & Validation

Keep contract managers, consultants, lawyers, planners and other specialists in the review loop before findings are relied upon for project or dispute decisions.

HOW WE APPROACH AI

Faster analysis. Traceable sources. Professional control.

01

Contract-aware

Analysis is framed around the governing contract, amendments, applicable procedures and the user’s role in the project.

02

Evidence-led

Important findings should be capable of being checked against the underlying contractual or project source.

03

Workflow-oriented

The objective is not simply to answer questions, but to support repeatable professional tasks and usable project outputs.

04

Professionally validated

AI-assisted outputs support, rather than replace, qualified legal, contractual, planning, quantum, technical or other specialist judgment.
