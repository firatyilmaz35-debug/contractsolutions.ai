THE CHALLENGE

Change becomes difficult to recover when scope, instruction and entitlement are not tracked together.

Variations often develop gradually through drawings, technical instructions, site correspondence and revised requirements. By the time valuation begins, the contractual trail may already be fragmented.

Variation & Change Orders brings the commercial and contractual record together so teams can identify scope change earlier and prepare it more consistently.
