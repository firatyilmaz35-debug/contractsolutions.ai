THE CHALLENGE

A change is only recoverable when scope, authority, procedure, evidence and impact remain connected.

Project change rarely arrives as a document labelled “Variation”. It develops through instructions, revised drawings, design development, access constraints, changed quantities, technical responses, omissions, acceleration requests and evolving Employer requirements.

The commercial problem starts when the team cannot show what changed from the contractual baseline, who had authority to instruct it, what notice or quotation procedure applied, which records prove the event, and how the change affected time or cost. A strong change-control process preserves that chain while the project is still live.

KEY CAPABILITIES

Turn fragmented change records into an auditable variation position.

01

Change Event Detection & Classification

Identify potential change events from instructions, revised drawings, correspondence, technical submissions, scope movement and other project records, then classify them for contractual review.

02

Instruction & Authority Review

Map who issued the direction, the contractual status of the instruction, relevant authority limits and whether confirmation, clarification or a formal instruction is required before the change is administered.

03

Scope & Baseline Comparison

Compare the changed requirement against the original scope, specifications, drawings, Employer’s Requirements, schedules, BoQ or other baseline documents to define the actual scope delta.

04

Notice & Procedure Compliance

Identify applicable notification, quotation, proposal, assessment and response procedures, including contractual deadlines and consequences under FIDIC, NEC or bespoke mechanisms.

05

Entitlement & Contractual Basis

Connect the factual change to relevant variation, compensation-event, instruction, change-in-law, unforeseeable-condition or other contractual provisions and record the basis for professional review.

06

Time & Cost Impact Interface

Link each change with affected programme activities, delay events, quantities, resources and cost records so planning and commercial specialists can develop the time and valuation position.

07

Valuation Framework

Structure applicable rates, new rates, quotations, build-ups, dayworks, quantities, supporting invoices and other valuation inputs without replacing the professional valuation or quantity-surveying judgment.

08

Change Status & Resolution Tracking

Maintain visibility from initial event through notice, instruction, quotation, submission, Engineer/Employer assessment, negotiation, agreement, determination and any subsequent dispute or reservation.

TYPICAL OUTPUTS

Outputs that show what changed, why it is compensable and what remains unresolved.

Each output is designed to preserve the contractual and evidentiary trail while giving contracts, commercial, planning and management teams a common working view.

01

Change Event Register

A live register of potential and confirmed changes with event date, source, status, responsible party, contractual basis, notice position, valuation status and key records.

02

Scope Delta Matrix

Original and changed requirements compared side by side, with references to drawings, specifications, quantities, instructions and relevant contract documents.

03

Instruction & Authority Matrix

Instructions and directions mapped to issuer, contractual authority, confirmation status and any procedural action needed to preserve the position.

04

Notice & Procedure Tracker

Applicable notice, quotation, proposal, assessment and response deadlines organised by change event, including outstanding actions and procedural risk.

05

Entitlement & Clause Note

A concise, source-referenced summary linking the factual event to the contractual provisions and identifying assumptions, reservations and issues requiring professional judgment.

06

Valuation Build-Up Schedule

Quantities, rates, resources, quotations, invoices and other cost inputs organised into a reviewable valuation structure for the commercial or quantum team.

07

Time / Programme Impact Interface

Affected milestones and programme activities, related delay events and required planning inputs identified so the change can be coordinated with any EOT or delay analysis.

08

Variation Submission Pack

A structured submission framework connecting event, instruction, scope delta, notices, entitlement, evidence, time implications, valuation and requested contractual action.
